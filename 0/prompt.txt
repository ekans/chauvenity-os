## The problem

- `scripts/sync-greeter.sh` copies a hardcoded list of five files: three in `chauvenity-greeter/` plus `chauvenity-lock/LockFace.qml` and `lock.mjs`.
- PR #23 makes `LockFace.qml` use new files: `chauvenity-lock/LockScene.qml`, `chauvenity-lock/Firefly.qml` and the sprites in `chauvenity-lock/art/` (`art/sprites.sh` regenerates them and doesn't need to ship).
- A sync against #23 with today's list copies the new `LockFace.qml` without those files. In a test, Quickshell then failed with `LockScene is not a type`, and the login screen didn't load at all. Nothing warns whoever runs the sync. That could be weeks from now.
- The sync only runs by hand ("Re-run after the greeter changes and commit the result"). Nothing in CI or the build calls it.

## What to decide and build

- Fixing it means the trap can't come back, not just that today's list gets longer. The list went stale silently once, and the next file added to the face would bring the trap back. Pick the structure yourself. Examples: derive what to copy from the dotfiles tree (everything the greeter loads, minus the lock's own shell.qml, lock.sh, tests and art/sprites.sh), or make the script refuse to finish unless the copied tree loads.
